Estimated AI API token cost only, calculated from the local rate table. Standard AI responses provide usage, not invoice-authoritative per-call cost; missing provider billing remains explicitly unreconciled. DeepSeek uses returned cache-hit/cache-miss counters when available; without a split, input is conservatively priced as cache miss. Provider-key tests and paid failed outputs are recorded. Separate search/enrichment-provider fees are excluded.

  

    What can spend DeepSeek tokens?
    
Recorded when an API response returns usage: Single and Batch CV parsing (including repair/continuation calls), CV blinding, JobAdder Create Profile parsing, CV Summary, CV Scoring, The Owl map and chat, AI Crawler Sourcing Notes & Scorecard, Blind JD and retries, Company Profile and retries, OneNote salary-component extraction, Lead Finder AI title expansion/classification/people/email searches, provider-key tests, and paid malformed or empty outputs.

    
No DeepSeek tokens: OCR/text extraction, PDFium/preview rendering, DOCX generation, JobAdder candidate search/result retrieval, AI Crawler deterministic Job Fit/result phase, sorting, Boolean highlighting, preview caching/prefetch, salary calculation after extraction, OAuth/connectivity, Outlook drafts, and local exports.

    
Not included: Tavily, SerpAPI, Apollo or other third-party credits/fees. Their billing status is reported separately when those providers are used. If a provider/network failure returns no usage object, exact tokens or charge cannot be known; CV Studio marks the paid call as ambiguous and records only usage actually returned by the provider.

    
Older history: records created before v24.6.215 keep their stored cost, but their token/call/cache breakdown cannot be reconstructed and is shown as Legacy · n/a.
